IRIX 6.5 colour schemes for mwc

The frame colours in docs/mwmrc.irix-schemes. Title text and shadows are mwc's, from Motif's formula: black text above 70% brightness, white otherwise. Where the palette gives other title text, its optional override is noted. The lines in the box go into ~/.config/mwc/mwmrc.
