## A. Defects and costs, medium

1. **Use after free when a monitor goes.** wlroots emits the output's destroy before it
   takes the output out of the layout. fifo's destroy listener, added after mwc's,
   releases held commits; a fullscreen window's commit asked `output_at()`, which found
   the output in the layout and returned the freed struct. [Fixed: `output_destroy()`
   clears `wlr_output->data` and removes the output from the layout itself. fifo.sh
   unplugs the monitor of a fullscreen `fifoc hold`; ASan reported it before.]
2. **The new repeat test could not fail.** It matched the `repeat 25 600` printed at
   startup. [Fixed: the events are cleared first.]
3. **One keymap per keyboard.** wlroots compares keymaps by pointer, so every switch
   between keyboard devices sent the keymap to every client and Xwayland. [Fixed: one
   shared keymap. session.sh: a second keyboard compiles none.]
4. **Dock frame as one buffer of the dock's size.** A docked client sets it: 550 MB
   for a wide and a tall tool. [Fixed: a rect and four bevel strips, pixel for pixel as
   before. dock.sh checks the peak memory.]
5. **`_NET_WM_STATE_STICKY` lost before mapping.** `read_initial_state()` knew five
   states. [Fixed. window.sh, five tries for the race.]
6. **Snap preview as one buffer of the target's size**, 130 MB at 8K, kept while hidden.
   [Fixed: four strips. snap.sh on an 8K output.]
7. **Move feedback drawn for every motion event**, with Pango, a buffer and an upload.
   [Fixed: through a 16 ms throttle. move.sh with `vinput glide`: 400 events, 2 draws.]
8. **X11 struts skipped the arrange throttle.** [Fixed: they use it. outputs.sh with
   `xclient strut_flicker`: 603 workarea changes before, 9 after.]
9. **A window going away took the keyboard from an on-demand layer surface**, and ended
   a panel popup's grab. [Fixed: the next window only becomes active when one unmaps
   or minimizes by itself; Lower and Alt+Tab still move the keyboard. layer.sh.]
10. **`useIconBox` off by reload while the box had the keyboard** left nothing focused.
    [Fixed. iconbox.sh.]
11. **Window sizes up to INT32_MAX** through wp_viewporter overflowed frame code.
    [Fixed: capped at 2^20. crash.sh under UBSan.]
