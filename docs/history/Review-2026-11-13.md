## Dropped

Reported by the review, but not bugs:

- A frame button held down can act on a hidden tab (`src/tabs.c:103`). The action is called,
  but does nothing on a hidden tab, so the click is only lost.
- A `WM_TRANSIENT_FOR` cycle might hang `view_restore` (`src/view.c:741`). wlroots refuses
  such a cycle ("would create a loop"), and the second parent had no effect when tried.
- The keyboard focus staying on an override-redirect window after it unmaps
  (`um_unmap()`). Xwayland destroys the surface of a window it unmaps, and the seat lets
  go of it; a test pins that.
