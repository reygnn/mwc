## Status

All points settled on the two branches. Each fix with a test that fails without it, checked by taking the change out, except where the point says otherwise.

1. **FIFO went by the fastest output, wlroots by the one most of the window is on** `src/fifo.c:153`, fifo, low-medium.

   The scene sends frame callbacks and presentation feedback only for a buffer's primary output, the enabled one it overlaps most (wlr_scene.c, surface.c in 0.19.2). A window mostly on a 60 Hz monitor with a strip on a 144 Hz one had its barrier cleared at 144 Hz while its frames came at 60: a client pacing by FIFO alone drew for nothing. A commit without damage schedules a frame only on the primary output, so on another one the barrier waited for the fallback timer.

   [Fixed: watch_output() takes the primary output of the surface's scene buffer, found in its view's tree (or the whole scene for a surface of no window); without a buffer in the scene yet, any output it is on. fifo.sh: across two monitors at 60 and 144 Hz, mostly on the fast one it goes at 144 (fails with the first of its outputs), mostly on the slow one at 60 (fails with the fastest).]

2. **An output of unknown refresh never won against a known one** `src/fifo.c:155`, fifo, low.

   [Settled with 1: the primary output doesn't go by the rate.]

8. **A grouped window given a parent on another desktop marks that desktop urgent** `src/tabs.c:249`, desktop, low.

9. **A rewrite in place within one timestamp tick, at the same size, isn't seen** `src/wallpaper.c:51`, wallpaper, low.

   With multigrain timestamps (Linux 6.13 and later on ext4, xfs, btrfs, tmpfs) mwc's own stat makes the next write fine-grained; on FAT or some NFS a second save within 2 s at the same size would be missed until the next change.

   [Left: CAVEATS has no place for so narrow a case; a rename, as editors and image tools save, always gives a new inode.]
