All points settled on test/invariant-matrix. A fix of the checker is shown by the self-test at the start of matrix.sh, which feeds it a broken state for each rule; the machinery's by the matrix and three walks of 1000 steps passing.

1. **The numbering went on over the cases, past the allDesktops rules** `tests/ops.sh:64`, machinery, high.

   ops_reset() kept SEQ: the matrix spawned c1 to c384, mwmrc had rules up to c200-all, and the app cases of the shaded and tiled-with-tabs starting points set an app_id no rule matched. That was the combination of 0cc7f90.

   [Fixed: ops_reset() starts the numbering over, mwmrc has rules up to c500, and op_spawn() fails beyond.]

2. **The usable-area check compared strings** `tests/invariants.awk:109`, checker, medium.

   field() returns strings, and gawk compared "100" < "30" as such: with a panel at the top, a cell below it was outside.

   [Fixed: every field is taken as a number.]

3. **The keyboard rule let every "other" pass** `tests/invariants.awk:57`, checker, medium.

   mwc gives the keyboard to a window's surface or a layer surface only; wlroots keeps it on the toplevel during a popup grab. "other" means a layer surface, and the escape hid the seat on one while no layer had the keys.

   [Fixed: a layer with the keys means the seat on a surface of no window and no window with the keyboard, and the other way round.]

4. **The sticky-dialog rule couldn't fail** `tests/invariants.awk:79`, checker, low.

   everywhere is view_on_all_desktops() at dump time, which takes the root's sticky already, and the root was looked up by app_id.

   [Fixed: replaced. Windows have an id in the state file, dialogs their root's; a dialog not everywhere is on its root's desktop, and one hidden with its window has one.]

5. **The layout's cells were chosen by visible** `tests/invariants.awk:100`, checker, low.

   [Fixed: by being on this desktop, as tile_arrange() does; a tiled window here must be in sight, no icon and not fullscreen.]

6. **The fill rule checked only the bounding box** `tests/invariants.awk:116`, checker, low.
